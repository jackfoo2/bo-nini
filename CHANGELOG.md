# Changelog

## v0.4.1 (2026-09-26)

- **Hold to read.** Resting the mouse on something Bo says keeps it open and in place until you move off. On phones, tap it to hold it and tap anywhere else to let go.

## v0.4.0 (2026-09-25)

- **Memory board.** Bo pins things you tell it to a corkboard in the kitchen, and may ask how one went after you've been away. Once per note, never as guilt. Stored only in your browser.
- **Bring your own AI key.** Live replies from Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Venice, a local Ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible API, streamed straight from the browser.
- **Honest energy.** Energy is thinking. With your own key or a local model, thinking doesn't cost Bo energy.

## Earlier versions (before this repo)

- **v0.3** A kitchen with a sink, stove, island, pantry and fridge. Three cooking classes from real recipes with a live recipe card. Head gadgets: a magnifying glass, a spyglass, and a chef hat. Kitchen tool arms.
- **v0.2** An expressive head that turns toward whatever Bo is working on.
- **v0.1** A studio apartment in a desktop window, a needs-based routine, felt time, energy and naps, and chat.
